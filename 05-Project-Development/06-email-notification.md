# Email Notification

## Objective

Send an automated email notification to the caller after the ticket is created and classified.

## Flow Designer Action

Action:

Send Email

## Email Configuration

### To

Caller → Email

### Subject

Your Request for the issue has been submitted.

### Body

Ticket confirmation message.

## Process

1. Ticket is created.
2. Flow Designer classifies the ticket.
3. Category and Subcategory are updated.
4. Email notification is sent to the caller.

## Expected Result

The caller receives an email confirming that the IT support request has been submitted.
